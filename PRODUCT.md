# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages from the public repo `jykalabs-dev/jykalabs-website`, served at the custom domain jykalabs.com. No build step. The contact form posts to Web3Forms (free tier), which emails each message to Jyka Labs; no address is shown on the site.

## Users

The company website of Jyka Labs. In order of priority (all confirmed by the founder):

1. **Companies that want a software product built**: business owners or leaders deciding whether Jyka Labs is a credible team to build with.
2. **Partners and investors**: people sizing up what the company does, what it has built and where it is heading.
3. **People who may join**: future hires and collaborators judging the work and the way it is done.

Future customers of the two products are not the primary audience of this site; each product will have its own site.

## Product Purpose

Present Jyka Labs as a software company that builds SaaS products, show the two products it is building, and let any visitor send a question through a contact form. Success: a visitor understands in seconds what Jyka Labs does and builds, finds it credible, and uses the form.

## Positioning

Confirmed by the founder:

- **Built for India's operating realities**: products designed for how Indian businesses actually work: Hindi and regional languages, low-cost phones, patchy networks, and Indian law (data protection, GST).
- **Practical software for unglamorous industries**: sectors that run on phone calls and paper (private security, cold chain) get proper tools.
- **A product company, not an agency**: Jyka Labs builds and runs its own products end to end.

Assumption to confirm: the founder also wants companies that need software built to get in touch. This is reconciled as "we build our own products, and bring the same product discipline to a few companies who want one built", not as an agency offering.

## Operating Context

- **OneMuster** (in development; shown as "Coming soon"): security and facility management for housing societies and workplaces. Security agencies plan duties, guards mark attendance with a selfie and location on a phone app that works offline (English, Hindi, Marathi), problems escalate through a chain of people on agreed timings, and monthly bills are prepared from the duties actually done, which the society can check. Societies can also look after equipment, service contracts and renewals.
- **ThandiMandi** (in development; shown as "Coming soon"): a refrigeration and cold-chain discovery platform for India. A directory of cold-chain companies, products, services and professionals (refrigeration, cold rooms, processing, logistics, maintenance); buyers post a requirement, the team screens it, and up to five screened companies respond; professionals show skills and certificates; guides and calculators. Its own words: "A discovery and information platform, not a marketplace."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No personal contact details anywhere: no email address, phone number, people's names or street address. Contact only through the form.
- No legal name (LLP) or location shown for now (founder's decision).
- Both products are unreleased: never describe them as available, never show made-up users, figures, logos, testimonials or screenshots of demo data.
- The form collects name, email, optional organisation and a message: the page needs a short notice of how that is used (DPDP Act 2023).
- Must work without JavaScript as far as possible; Pages serves static files only.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Jyka Labs** (domain jykalabs.com). No existing logo, palette or typeface for the company; the products have their own brands, which this site doesn't reuse.
- Voice (from the founder): minimalist, simple, eye-catching, very professional. Plain, specific English.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product facts above, from the OneMuster repos (`~/Documents/Apps/jykalabs-1m/`) and ThandiMandi (`~/Documents/Apps/ThandiMandi`).
- No customers, testimonials, press, metrics, team names or photos exist to show. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Say what is true and nothing more: unreleased products are "coming soon", no invented proof.
2. One clear way to reach us: the form.
3. Show the products through what they do, in plain words, not through hype.
4. Credibility through craft: the site itself is evidence of the quality of work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible focus, full keyboard use, labelled form fields with clear errors, reduced-motion respected. Readable on low-cost phones and slow connections (small page weight, no heavy scripts or video).
