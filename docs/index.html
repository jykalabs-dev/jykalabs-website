What we do

We cover the whole life of a product: research, design, engineering, launch and day-to-day operation. The team that builds the software also runs it.

  • Software and apps

    Software development
    Custom software, written to be maintained and changed over time.
    Web and mobile apps
    Apps that work on low-cost phones and slow networks, in the languages people use.

  • Platforms and marketplaces

    SaaS and platforms
    Subscription software that serves many organisations from one secure system.
    Marketplaces and communities
    Platforms that connect buyers, sellers and professionals.

  • Data and automation

    Reports and dashboards from the data a business already has, and automation of routine, repetitive tasks.
