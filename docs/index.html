Our own products, end to end
We are not an agency. We design, build, ship and run each product ourselves, and keep improving it after launch.

OneMuster

For security agencies, housing societies and workplaces

Security and facility management, from the gate to the monthly bill.

  • Agencies plan duties and see who is on post, site by site.
  • Guards mark attendance with a selfie and their location, even with no network, on a phone app in English, Hindi and Marathi.
  • A missed check or an absent guard goes up a named chain of people until someone acts.
  • Bills come from the duties actually done, and the society can check them before paying.

ThandiMandi

For refrigeration and cold chain businesses across India

A discovery and information platform for the cold chain, not a marketplace.

  • Find companies, products, services and professionals: refrigeration, cold rooms, processing, logistics and maintenance.
  • Post what you need; it is screened, and up to five screened companies respond.
  • Professionals show their skills and certificates and apply for jobs.
  • Guides and calculators for the people who design and run cold rooms.

How we work

Built for the person doing the work
The guard, the supervisor, the technician. Screens are plain, buttons say what they do, and nothing depends on a perfect connection.
Privacy and security from the first version
Consent, encryption, data export and deletion are designed in, so customers do not have to ask for them later.
Honest numbers
Our products show what happened, from real records. If a figure isn't measured, we don't show it.
